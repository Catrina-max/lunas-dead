# The Seven Spaces

The Seven Spaces organize both narrative progression and different ways of knowing.

## 1. Merry Scary

**Question:** What is an object?

A gallery / store / archive environment where commerce, art, memory, and anomaly overlap.

## 2. Memorial

**Question:** How does memory assign value?

Names, offerings, photographs, and stories make relational networks visible.

## 3. Zephyr's Garage

**Question:** What happens when evidence and representation diverge?

The player learns environmental grammar and contradiction.

## 4. Veil

**Question:** Can perception be engineered?

A corporate system transforms a philosophical problem into a question of ownership, control, and infrastructure.

## 5. Deep Downtown

**Question:** Is spatial continuity itself an interface?

A familiar transportation metaphor becomes increasingly impossible.

## 6. Palacio

**Question:** Can multiple representations coexist?

Memory, performance, image, fashion, and the society of the dead intersect.

## 7. Ofrenda

**Question:** Does meaning emerge through relationships?

Objects encountered earlier return with radically changed significance because their relational network has changed.
