# Contributing

Luna's Dead is **not currently an open-contribution story universe**.

Please do not submit unsolicited:

- character concepts;
- story continuations;
- screenplay pages;
- worldbuilding;
- garment designs;
- music intended for incorporation;
- logos;
- mystery solutions;
- derivative artwork intended as official project material.

This boundary protects both the project and outside creators from ambiguity about authorship and ownership.

Technical discussion, bug reports, research references, or specifically invited contributions may be accepted through defined project channels.

Formal creative collaboration begins with a scoped written agreement.
