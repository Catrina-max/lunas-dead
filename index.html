ABOUT

A story world built as a research system.

Luna's Dead is an evolving interactive story world and art–technology experiment about perception, memory, evidence, love, death, and the instability of what appears to be real.

At its center is Luna, an attorney who survives a near-death event and begins perceiving contradictions between the ordinary world and another representational layer associated with the dead.

This site documents the public research, design systems, diagrams, and selected experiments behind the project. The complete story bible and unreleased canon remain private.

WORKING QUESTION

When every observer sees through an interface, how do we decide what deserves to be believed?

EXPERIMENTAL MYSTERY

The Room Doesn't Fit.

An early playable investigation takes place in a preserved 1980s motorcycle garage associated with Zephyr and Los Muertos.

The room contains overlapping versions: police reconstruction, physical traces, memory, archival records, ghost testimony, and Luna's case theory.

The player is not rewarded for pretending to know more than the evidence supports.
