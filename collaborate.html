COLLABORATION

Create an artifact that belongs inside the world.

Fashion, motorcycles, objects, sound, photography, film, installation, and other disciplines can enter Luna's Dead through defined collaborations.

Fictional Artifact→Game Asset→Screen Object→Gallery Object→Physical Edition

BOUNDARY

Scoped collaboration, not open-universe authorship.

Collaborators receive the material necessary for their defined project, not automatic access to the entire private canon.

Any substantive collaboration should distinguish Luna's Dead background IP, collaborator background IP, and newly created material.

Ownership, licenses, approvals, credit, compensation, confidentiality, and canon status should be resolved in writing.
