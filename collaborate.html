SECTION 05 / COLLABORATION FRAMEWORK

Scoped collaboration across a shared story world.

The purpose of this section is to document how outside artists, designers and producers can contribute without collapsing the distinction between background IP, collaborator IP and new project work.

5.1 / OBJECT MODEL

An artifact can exist in several media.

Fictional artifact→Game asset→Screen object→Gallery object→Physical edition

The collaboration model begins from worldbuilding rather than conventional merchandise. A riding jacket, photograph, motorcycle, piece of jewelry or memorial object can have narrative and evidentiary functions before it becomes a physical product.

5.2 / IP LAYERS
ALuna's Dead background IPExisting story, characters, names, worldbuilding, scripts, game concepts and approved visual material.
BCollaborator background IPMaterial, marks, patterns, methods or designs the collaborator already owns.
CNew collaboration materialWork created specifically through the collaboration and governed by the relevant written agreement.
5.3 / ACCESS
WORKING RULECollaborators receive the story material necessary for their defined contribution, not automatic access to the entire canon.

A Zephyr fashion collaborator may need approved visual direction, period context, material vocabulary and intended uses. They generally do not need the complete mystery solution, future series arc or unrelated private character files.

5.4 / DOCUMENTATION

Before substantive production, the parties should address ownership, licenses, permitted media, manufacturing, retail, game and screen use, exhibition, approvals, compensation, credit, confidentiality, prototypes, portfolio use, termination and treatment of unused concepts.
