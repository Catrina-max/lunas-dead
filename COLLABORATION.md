# Collaboration Framework

Luna's Dead is being developed as a world in which game design, film, visual art, fashion, objects, music, and physical environments can intersect.

The project is open to **scoped collaboration**.

It is not an open-source fictional universe.

## Three Layers of IP

A collaboration should distinguish:

### Luna's Dead Background IP

Existing story, characters, names, fictional organizations, scripts, visual material, game concepts, and worldbuilding.

### Collaborator Background IP

Material the collaborator created or owned before the collaboration.

### New Collaboration Material

New work created specifically for the project.

Those categories should be documented before significant production begins.

---

## Object-Based Collaboration

One of the project's preferred approaches is to create objects that feel as though they existed inside the fictional world before they became commercial products.

Examples might include:

- riding jackets;
- club garments;
- jewelry;
- motorcycles;
- photographs;
- archival ephemera;
- memorial objects;
- costumes;
- furniture;
- sound objects;
- props.

A successful object may become:

```text
worldbuilding
    ↓
game asset
    ↓
film / performance object
    ↓
gallery artifact
    ↓
physical edition
```

---

## Controlled Story Access

Collaborators should receive only the story material necessary for their work.

A clothing collaborator may need:

- approved visual direction;
- cultural / historical context;
- material vocabulary;
- character function;
- intended use.

They usually do not need:

- complete mystery solutions;
- future series arcs;
- unrelated character histories;
- confidential business plans.

---

## Before Production

A written agreement should address, as applicable:

- background IP;
- newly created IP;
- assignment or licensing;
- permitted media;
- manufacturing;
- retail;
- game use;
- film / series use;
- gallery use;
- promotional use;
- exclusivity;
- compensation;
- approvals;
- credit;
- confidentiality;
- prototypes;
- portfolio rights;
- termination;
- treatment of unused concepts.

Participation in a defined collaboration does not by itself confer ownership in the broader Luna's Dead story world.
