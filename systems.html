A room can be evidence before any object inside it becomes a clue.

2.4 / INFORMATION

Surprise is not truth.

I(x) = −log₂ P(x)

The project uses information theory as a conceptual aid: a highly expected observation carries less surprise than an unlikely one. But narrative significance cannot be reduced to the equation.

PROPOSITION 03An anomaly earns attention, not automatic belief.
METHOD NOTE

The core system goal is epistemic restraint. The player should become better at distinguishing what was seen from what was inferred. Luna's legal training gives this distinction narrative weight: the supernatural expands the evidentiary field without abolishing standards of reasoning.
