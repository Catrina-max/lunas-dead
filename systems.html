SYSTEMS

The player never receives a truth layer.

Luna's Dead is being designed around the difference between observation, evidence, interpretation, and conclusion.

Perception Layers

The same underlying object may render differently through living perception, memory, ghost testimony, institutional reconstruction, or Veil systems.

Case Theory

Observations become evidence only in relation to disputed questions. Evidence supports hypotheses, contradictions, and provisional conclusions.

Information Value

Unexpected details can force the player to revise a model. Surprise is informative, but not equivalent to truth.

Memory Networks

Names, objects, photographs, ritual, testimony, and place form relational structures rather than a single archive.

CASE THEORY

Reasoning as gameplay.

The system should distinguish six states:

Observation → Evidence → Inference → Contradiction → Hypothesis → Conclusion

The game rewards disciplined uncertainty. “Official Account Incomplete” can be a meaningful victory even when the player still cannot say exactly what happened.
